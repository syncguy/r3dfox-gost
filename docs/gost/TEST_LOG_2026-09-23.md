### L. External STUN / NAT discovery — PASS

External STUN gathering was tested. Raw candidates are not retained in the published log.

Results:

- Cloudflare STUN over UDP/3478 produced a server-reflexive (`typ srflx`) candidate;
- Yandex `stun.rtc.yandex.net` over UDP/3478 produced a server-reflexive (`typ srflx`) candidate;
- Yandex on port 443 did not produce `srflx` in this environment;
- a Google public STUN endpoint did not produce `srflx` in this environment.

The failed individual endpoints do not establish a browser defect because public STUN reachability may be affected by network/operator filtering. The successful Cloudflare and Yandex UDP/3478 results establish that the physical-XP build can execute an external STUN transaction, discover the NAT mapping and gather a `srflx` candidate.

No local address, public/NAT-mapped address, related address, candidate port, or NAT-mapped port is included here.

**Conclusion:** external STUN PASS; `srflx` gathering PASS; UDP NAT discovery PASS.
